Overview

Through Pasadena City College's machining program, I trained on manual lathes, mills, and CNC equipment — producing precision parts to tolerance and earning the program's Top Machinist distinction. This hands-on experience directly informs how I design parts in CAD, since I understand what's actually machinable versus what only looks correct on screen.
