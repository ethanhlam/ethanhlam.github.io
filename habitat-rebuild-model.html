Post-Fire Rebuild Model — Habitat for Humanity

A 3/8" scale interactive architectural model built for Altadena families displaced by fire, developed with San Gabriel Valley Habitat for Humanity under a lead architect's mentorship.

Role
Model Builder / Volunteer
Organization
SGV Habitat for Humanity
Scale
3/8" = 1'-0"
Mentorship
Lead Architect

Overview

Following the Altadena wildfires, San Gabriel Valley Habitat for Humanity needed a physical model to communicate a proposed rebuild design to displaced families and stakeholders. I built a 3/8" scale interactive model of the home, working directly under a lead architect to translate architectural drawings into an accurate, presentable physical form.

Process

  • Reviewed architectural drawings and floor plans with the lead architect to establish scale, materials, and level of detail.
  • Built the model to be interactive — removable or openable elements to show interior layout, not just an exterior shell.
  • Worked to a community-facing deadline, balancing craftsmanship with the practical needs of a presentation model.

Impact

The model gave fire-displaced families and community stakeholders a tangible way to understand the proposed rebuild — something a 2D drawing set couldn't communicate on its own.
