Sheet 00 / About

From the mill to the model.

I'm an incoming Civil Engineering student at UC Berkeley, also completing coursework at Pasadena City College and working with the City of Temple City. My background is hands-on first — lathe, mill, and CNC machining — which shapes how I approach design: everything I model has to actually be manufacturable.

I'm currently leaning toward a shift into Mechanical Engineering, driven by an independent SOLIDWORKS certification track, personal CAD projects, and a growing portfolio of physical prototypes — including an underactuated robotic hand and a 3/8-inch scale interactive model built with San Gabriel Valley Habitat for Humanity for fire-displaced families in Altadena.

I care about the gap between a drawing and a working part — and closing it myself, on the machine, whenever I can.
